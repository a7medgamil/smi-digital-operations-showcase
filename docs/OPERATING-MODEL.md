# SMI — Eight-Domain Operating Model

## Purpose

SMI is organised as an education institution with connected academic, learner, people, performance, administrative and executive functions. The portfolio operating model groups **17 named operating areas into eight domains**.

This document describes the institution's operating scope. It is not a claim that every function is a separate application, fully automated, or independently verified in production.

## The eight domains

| # | Operating domain | Areas within the domain |
|---:|---|---|
| 01 | **Student Operations** | Student administration; attendance; scholarships |
| 02 | **Academic Operations** | English programs; course operations; scheduling and secondary sessions |
| 03 | **Teacher & Staff Operations** | Teacher and staff administration |
| 04 | **Assessment & Performance** | Assessments and examinations; grades and certificates; progress tracking |
| 05 | **Mental Training** | Structured mental training program |
| 06 | **Reporting & Analytics** | Progress reports; automation |
| 07 | **Administration** | Management console; notifications |
| 08 | **Executive & Financial Oversight** | Executive dashboard; financial and operational analytics |

## How the domains connect

- **Student Operations** maintains the learner-facing records and attendance context.
- **Academic Operations** defines the programs, courses and delivery schedule.
- **Teacher & Staff Operations** supports the people responsible for delivery.
- **Assessment & Performance** records learning checks, results, certificates and progress.
- **Mental Training** is a distinct structured program within the institution's learning model.
- **Reporting & Analytics** turns operational activity into recurring management information.
- **Administration** supports institutional settings, notifications and day-to-day oversight.
- **Executive & Financial Oversight** brings financial and operational information into an institution-wide view.

## Relationship to the application inventory

The separate [12-item Digital Application Inventory](SYSTEM-INVENTORY.md) names public applications, learner-facing platforms and staff-facing modules.

The two records answer different questions:

- **Operating model:** how the institution's work is organised across eight domains and 17 areas.
- **Application inventory:** which 12 named applications and modules are recorded, where they sit, and what public route evidence exists.

The portfolio data marks 12 of the 17 operating areas as system-supported. That classification does not mean there are 12 additional deployments, nor does it establish that every mapped function is fully automated or live.

## Evidence and privacy boundaries

- This is an institutional operating model based on the portfolio's supplied SMI scope.
- It is a functional grouping, not a verified org chart, data-flow diagram, software architecture or deployment register.
- No internal administrative URLs, credentials, learner records, staff records or database exports are published.
- Public route checks are documented separately in the application inventory and confirm HTTP reachability only.
